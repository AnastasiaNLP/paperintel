# Rubric: Synthesis Justification

Evaluate whether synthesis next steps and conclusions are justified by supplied
durable artifacts.

## Inputs

- selected paper ids
- durable `PaperWorkspace` artifacts
- optional comparison context
- synthesis report and rendered response

## Scoring

Score from `0.0` to `1.0`:

- `1.0`: next steps and conclusions are well supported by supplied evidence.
- `0.75`: mostly justified but misses one material caveat.
- `0.50`: plausible but generic or weakly tied to evidence.
- `0.25`: several recommendations are unsupported or overstated.
- `0.0`: recommendations conflict with evidence or rely on outside knowledge.

## Criteria

The judge should reward concrete caveats about missing benchmarks, readiness
gaps, and implementation uncertainty.
